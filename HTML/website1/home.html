DEPARTMENT OF CSE (Computer Science and Engineering )

The Department of Computer Science and Engineering at Ellenki College of Engineering and Technology was established with a vision to contribute to the IT sector and produce skilled Software Engineers for the global market. Over the years, especially in the last 15 years during the IT revolution, the department has made remarkable progress and is now considered one of the best departments in the college. The department’s primary focus is to equip students with modern computer techniques and prepare them for the rapidly evolving technological advancements. Computer Science is the flagship branch of Engineering, and the department emphasizes scientific research, scientific programming, and software engineering to foster the development of skilled professionals.
