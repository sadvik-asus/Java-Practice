Department of CSE

Established in 1986, the Department of Computer Science and Systems Engineering is dedicated to delivering top-tier education and pioneering research in the field of computer science. The department equips students with cutting-edge skills in computer science and engineering. Additionally, the department collaborates with industry leaders like Tata Consultancy Services (TCS) to offer the BTech Computer Science and Business Systems (CSBS) programme, ensuring students are well-prepared for the evolving demands of the tech industry.
